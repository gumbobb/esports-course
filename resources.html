Organizations and references used in this course

  • NASEF, the Network of Academic and Scholastic Esports Federations: free scholastic esports curriculum, clubs, and career resources.
  • High School Esports League (HSEL): national high school competition.
  • PlayVS: scholastic esports platform partnered with many state associations.
  • Liquipedia: the esports encyclopedia; tournaments, teams, players, formats.
  • Esports Earnings: prize pool and earnings data.
  • Esports Insider: industry news and business coverage.
  • OBS Studio: free broadcast and recording software.
  • Challonge and start.gg: free bracket and tournament management.
  • Twitch and YouTube Gaming: streaming platforms studied in Weeks 10 and 11.
  • Creative Commons licenses: how to use and credit images and media legally.

Course design notes

This course follows the Quality Matters general standards for online course design: a start-here module, measurable objectives aligned to every assessment, varied and accessible materials, planned interaction with content, instructor, and peers, stated feedback timelines, and learner support on every page. It applies Universal Design for Learning through choice in every assignment (engagement), content in text and video with transcripts (representation), and written, audio, video, and visual submission options (expression).

Capstone · 25% of the course grade

Capstone: Program or Event Proposal

One real, complete proposal you could hand to a principal, an athletic director, a sponsor, or a venue. You choose which kind. It is built in three checkpoints during the semester and finished in Week 15.

Choose your track

Track A: Program proposal

A plan to start or expand a scholastic esports program: mission, structure, roster plan, facilities and equipment, budget, wellness policy, and first-season calendar.

Track B: Event proposal

A plan for a one-day or weekend tournament: game and format, rules, schedule, venue or online logistics, staffing, budget, broadcast plan, and marketing plan.

Format choices

The proposal can be a written document (8 to 12 pages), a slide deck with speaker notes (18 to 25 slides), or a website. The presentation can be a recorded video, a live presentation in office hours, or narrated slides with captions. Pick the format that shows your work best. All formats are graded with the same rubric.

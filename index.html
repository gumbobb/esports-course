Welcome

Introduction to Esports Management

Competitive gaming is a real industry with real jobs: coaches, event managers, broadcasters, marketers, analysts, and program directors. In fifteen weeks you will learn how the industry works, how a team and a program are run, how an event gets planned and broadcast, and how to build a career in it. You will finish with a complete program or event proposal you can show to anyone.

A message from your instructor

Hi, I'm Mr. Hughes, and I'll be your instructor this semester. I have coached scholastic esports, run tournaments, and spent years in business and operations before teaching, so this course is built from the parts of the industry I have actually worked in. My job here is to guide, give you feedback quickly, and keep the course organized so you always know what to do next. Your job is to show up every week, try things, and ask when you are stuck.

This is an online course. Everything you need is on this site. Each week opens on Monday and follows the same pattern: learn, check, do, discuss, assess. Watch the welcome video, then go to Module 0.

Course welcome and site tour, captioned.

What you will be able to do by the end

1. Explain how the esports industry is structured and how money and attention move through it.
2. Design the structure, roles, and practice plan for a scholastic esports team or program.
3. Plan a competitive event from format and rules through budget, staffing, and run of show.
4. Produce a basic live broadcast segment and a marketing plan for an event or program.
5. Evaluate ethical, wellness, and governance issues in esports and recommend responsible practices.
6. Build a career plan and a sponsorship or program proposal suitable for a real audience.
